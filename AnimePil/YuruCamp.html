YURU CAMP

Yuru-Camp or Laid-Back Camp (Japanese: ゆるキャン△, Hepburn: Yurukyan) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Afro [ja]. Set in and around Yamanashi, Japan, the series chronicles the adventures of Rin Shima, Nadeshiko Kagamihara, and their friends as they travel to various campsites across the country.

Motosuko Observation Park

About

The Motosuko Observation Park, where Rin Shima went to in Season 1 EP1. Its actually a small parking lot, where there's a deidacted platform for people to look across Lake Motosuko onto Mount Fuji.

It is also very close to another location that appear in the anime, which is the Koan Camping Ground.

Koan Guest House

About

Part of the Koan Camping Ground, just beside Motosuko Observation Park. Its the camping ground Rin Shima went for camping in Season 1 EP1, also where Rin Shima first meet Nadeshiko Kagamihara.

The Guest House had officially collab with Yuru Camp IP, so they also sell some Yuru Camp Merch, aside from their main business.
