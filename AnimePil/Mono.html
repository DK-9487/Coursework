MONO

Mono is a Japanese four-panel manga series written and illustrated by Afro. Set in and around Yamanashi Prefecture, Satsuki Amamiya joins her school's Photography Club along with her best friend An Kiriyama after being inspired by Club President and senior student Makinohara, who she adored. To fulfill their club activities, the club and Haruno explore new places, capture a variety of photos with different kinds of cameras, experience the local cuisine and embark on other endeavours, not strictly related to photography.
