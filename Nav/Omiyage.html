Railway Merch

Train stations are souvenir hubs. General Store Railyard sells keychains that play station departure melodies and keychains shaped like station name boards, train themed snack, and station NewDays shops carry train goods from around ¥200, though not every shop stocks them.

Stationery

Stationery is a cheap, useful gift. Itoya in Ginza is a multi-story haven for collectors, Loft is colorful and good for gifts, and Hands suits functional tools.

Anime Figure

Akihabara has walls of gachapon machines, and Nakano Broadway is the place for vintage and rare finds. Mandarake Complex in Akihabara has eight floors of secondhand goods, so it’s good for hunting older series.

Manga & Light Novels

Used volumes at Book Off can cost as little as ¥100, and Kinokuniya’s Shinjuku South store also stocks English-translated manga. Most titles in stores are in Japanese, so check the language before buying.
